# 🔊 DIY Bluetooth Speaker with NFC & 3D Printed Enclosure

A custom-built Bluetooth speaker featuring wireless audio playback, NFC-based Spotify launching, and a 3D-printed enclosure. This project combines audio electronics, power management, soldering, 3D design, and smart automation into one compact DIY speaker.

## 🚀 Project Overview

This project started as a simple DIY Bluetooth speaker and evolved into a customized audio system with additional smart features.

he speakeTr uses the **DWCT14 Bluetooth audio module** for wireless music playback. An NFC tag is integrated into the enclosure, allowing users to tap their NFC-enabled smartphone to trigger Spotify.

The enclosure is custom-designed and 3D-printed to accommodate the electronics and speaker components.

## ✨ Features

- 🎵 **Bluetooth Audio:** Wireless music playback using the DWCT14 module.
- 📱 **NFC Integration:** Tap your smartphone on the NFC tag to launch Spotify.
- 🖨️ **Custom 3D-Printed Enclosure:** Designed and printed a dedicated speaker case.
- 🔋 **Power Management:** Integrated battery charging and voltage boosting circuits.
- 🔧 **Custom Electronics Assembly:** Combined audio, charging, and power circuits into a compact setup.
- 🎨 **Custom Design:** Personalized enclosure and component arrangement.

## ⚙️ Working Principle

1. The battery supplies power to the system.
2. The charging module manages battery charging.
3. The boost converter provides the required operating voltage.
4. The DWCT14 module receives audio wirelessly through Bluetooth.
5. The audio signal is amplified and delivered to the speaker driver.
6. The NFC tag stores an NFC-triggered action that opens Spotify on a compatible smartphone.
7. The custom 3D-printed enclosure houses and protects the complete assembly.

## 📱 NFC Integration

One of the interesting additions to this project is NFC integration.

The NFC tag is programmed with a Spotify launch action or supported Spotify link.

**How it works:**

- Enable NFC on your smartphone, if required.
- Bring the phone close to the NFC tag.
- The phone detects the tag.
- Spotify opens through the configured link or automation.

This feature makes accessing music quicker and adds a smart interaction to a regular Bluetooth speaker.

*Compatibility depends on the smartphone, NFC support, and configured tag action.*

## 🖨️ 3D Printed Enclosure
